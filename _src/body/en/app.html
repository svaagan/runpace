RunPace for iPhone

The same running pace calculator as on this site, but in your pocket and offline — plus three things the website does not have: race time predictions across distances, training paces from your VO₂max, and miles for those who run in them.

Free, no account, no sign-in. Everything is calculated on your phone.

What the app does

Calculator

Pace and speed on two linked wheels — drag one and the other follows. Below them, finish times for every common distance, or splits for typical interval distances.

Race plan

Set a distance and a goal time, get the pace you need and an even split table. The same as the split tool here, only out on the course with you.

Race times

Enter one result, get predictions for all the other distances, based on Riegel's formula. If you have a VO₂max in Apple Health, the app can use that as a starting point instead.

Training paces

VDOT-based zones — easy, marathon, threshold, interval and repetition — set automatically from your VO₂max, with a twelve-month trend line. This is not on the website.

Made for runners

  • Kilometres or miles — switch once and the whole app follows
  • Light, dark or system appearance
  • English and Norwegian
  • No account and no sign-in — your numbers stay on your device
  • Works offline, including where the signal fails on race day

What does it cost?

The app is free, with one small banner ad at the bottom. A single purchase removes it for good, on every device you use with the same Apple Account. There is no subscription and nothing locked behind payment — the whole calculator works whether or not you pay anything.

If you had the app before the ads arrived, you never see them. That is decided once, at first launch, and it is permanent. The details are in the privacy policy.

Requirements

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later. There is no Android version yet — there you can add the website to your home screen instead, and it behaves like an app with its own icon and full screen.
