Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

runpace.no uses no cookies and stores no personal data. The iOS app collects nothing about you — with one exception: an ad banner that some users see, described at the bottom.

Who is responsible

RunPace (runpace.no) is operated by a private individual. Contact: kontakt@runpace.no

What we collect on the website

RunPace uses no cookies. The site uses a custom-built, self-hosted analytics system that records the following on each page visit:

  • Timestamp of the visit
  • Anonymised IP address — your IP address is hashed (one-way encrypted) and stored only as a 16-character code. It is not possible to reconstruct the original address.
  • Browser and device (user-agent) — used to distinguish mobile from desktop
  • Traffic source — which page you came from (referrer), if available

No personal data such as name, email or identifiable data is stored. The data is used solely to understand aggregate traffic and improve the site.

Storage

Anonymised analytics data is stored on a private server in Norway. Data is not shared with third parties and is not sold.

Your rights (GDPR)

For this website, no personal data is stored, so there is in practice nothing to delete or export. For the iOS app, the ad data described under «Ads in the iOS app» is held by Google, and requests about it are best directed to Google — but you are welcome to contact kontakt@runpace.no either way, and we will help where we can.

Cookies

RunPace uses no cookies. No cookies are set in your browser, and you do not need to consent to anything when visiting the site.

The iOS app

The same calculator is available as an iOS app, published on the App Store as RunPace by the same developer. The app has no account and no login, it does not use the analytics described above, and every calculation happens on your device. The app itself collects nothing about you. The one exception is the ad banner described below, which is served by Google and only appears for some users.

Apple Health: If you choose to, RunPace can read the latest VO₂max value (cardio fitness) from Apple Health to calculate training zones and a starting point for race prediction. The value is used only on your device at that moment, and is never stored or sent anywhere — not to us and not to third parties. Access to Apple Health is optional; all features of the app work without it. You can turn off access at any time in the app's settings, or in iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Health data is never used for advertising. Your VO₂max value does not leave your device and is never passed to the ad network, in any form.

Ads in the iOS app

From version 1.5, RunPace shows a small banner ad at the bottom of the screen. The ads are delivered by Google AdMob and are what keeps the app free.

Who sees ads: only users whose first download was version 1.5 or later. If you installed RunPace before ads existed, your app stays ad-free permanently and at no cost — the ad software is never started for you, and no request is made on your behalf. New users can remove the banner for good with a one-time in-app purchase. Once the banner is removed, the ad software is no longer started and stops sending anything.

What Google receives: when a banner is loaded, Google's ad software collects and processes data about the device and the ad, on our behalf and as an independent controller. This typically includes:

  • Device and advertising identifiers — including the IDFA advertising identifier if you allow tracking (see below)
  • Device and app information — device model, operating system version, language, coarse location derived from the IP address, and the app version
  • Ad interaction data — that an ad was shown, viewed or tapped
  • Diagnostics — crash and performance data from the ad software

This data is not linked to a name, an email address or an account with us. We never receive it in identifiable form; we only see aggregate revenue figures in AdMob. How Google uses the data is described in Google's privacy policy for partner sites and apps.

Tracking permission (ATT): the first time an ad is about to load, iOS asks whether RunPace may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you say no, the IDFA is not shared and the ads you see are non-personalised. The app works exactly the same either way. You can change your answer at any time in iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland: before the first ad loads, you are shown a consent form from Google that asks whether your data may be used for personalised advertising. Your choice is stored on the device and can be changed again at any time from the app's settings.

Legal basis: personalised advertising is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time through the choices above. Non-personalised advertising, which still needs limited device data to serve and count an ad and to prevent fraud, is based on our legitimate interest in financing a free app.

If you would rather not see ads at all, you can buy the one-time ad removal in the app's settings, or contact kontakt@runpace.no.
