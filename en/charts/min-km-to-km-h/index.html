min/km to km/h conversion chart

Divide 60 by your pace in minutes to get the speed in km/h. 5:00 min/km becomes 60 ÷ 5 = 12.0 km/h. The other direction is the same sum: 60 ÷ 12 = 5:00 min/km.

If your pace has seconds, convert them to decimals first. 5:30 is 5.5 minutes, and 60 ÷ 5.5 = 10.9 km/h.

The formula

  • min/km to km/h: 60 ÷ pace in minutes = km/h
  • km/h to min/km: 60 ÷ km/h = pace in minutes

The trap is the seconds. 5:30 min/km is not 5.30 minutes but 5.5 — because 30 seconds is half of the minute, not thirty hundredths of it. Divide the seconds by 60 before you go any further.

Why two units for the same thing?

Pace in min/km answers the question runners actually ask themselves mid-race: how long will the next kilometre take? Speed in km/h answers a question machines ask: how far do I get in an hour? That is why your watch shows pace and the treadmill shows speed — and why you keep having to convert between them.
